## Trademark note

These are photographs *of* branded events (US Open, Formula 1, New York Fashion
Week). The licences above settle copyright, not trademark: a commercial page
offering transport *for* a named event can imply an affiliation that does not
exist. Flagged to AD 11-aug-2026 — pending a decision on whether the client is
comfortable with it or wants generic imagery instead.
