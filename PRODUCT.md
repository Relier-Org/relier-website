# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Executive travelers, assistants and concierges, and event organizers who need to arrange premium ground transportation quickly and with confidence.

## Product Purpose

Relier presents its global personal mobility concierge service and turns qualified visits into reservations. Success means the booking flow feels organic, easy to interpret, and functionally effortless across devices.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and exclusive. The experience should feel precise and composed, especially while users provide itinerary and passenger information.

## Anti-references

Avoid the generic limousine-company look, excessive gold, decorative luxury cues, cluttered forms, unclear hierarchy, and inconsistent alignment. The reservation flow must never feel improvised or visually disordered.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action immediately understandable.
2. Earn trust through order, consistency, and predictable behavior.
3. Keep premium expression restrained and functional.
4. Preserve clarity across desktop and mobile breakpoints.
5. Reduce friction without hiding necessary information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and interaction standards. Maintain visible focus states, readable labels, generous touch targets, semantic controls, and support for reduced motion.
