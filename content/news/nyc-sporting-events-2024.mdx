### 1. Big East Men’s Basketball Tournament | Date: March 13-16, 2024

The Big East Men’s Basketball Tournament is a thrilling event held at Madison Square Garden, featuring top college basketball teams competing for conference supremacy. Witness intense matchups, buzzer-beaters, and memorable moments as teams battle for the coveted championship title and a ticket to the NCAA Tournament.

### 2. New York Knicks and Brooklyn Nets NBA Games | Date: January 23, March 23, and April 12,  2024

Immerse yourself in the excitement of the NBA as the New York Knicks take the court at Madison Square Garden and the Brooklyn Nets showcase their skills at the Barclays Center. Experience the adrenaline-pumping atmosphere, skillful plays, and the thrill of live professional basketball.

### 3. NYCFC and New York Red Bulls MLS Matches | Date: May 18, 2024

Enjoy the passion and precision of [Major League Soccer](https://www.mlssoccer.com/) as New York City FC (NYCFC) and the New York Red Bulls face off in a riveting match. Feel the energy of the crowd and witness the dynamic play of these top-tier soccer teams.

### 4. Westminster Kennel Club Dog Show | Date: May 13, 2024

The Westminster Kennel Club Dog Show is a premier canine event showcasing the best in breed. Attend this prestigious dog show at Madison Square Garden and witness the grace and charm of top dogs competing for prestigious titles.

### 5. Belmont Stakes | Date: June 8, 2024

The Belmont Stakes, held at Belmont Park, is the third and final leg of the Triple Crown. Join the excitement as thoroughbred horses and jockeys compete for glory in this prestigious horse racing event.

### 6. New York Yankees and New York Mets MLB Games | Date: July 23, 2024

Experience the magic of baseball at its finest with MLB games featuring the New York Yankees and the New York Mets. Whether at the historic Yankee Stadium or the modern Citi Field, enjoy the thrill of America’s pastime in the heart of the city.

### 7. US Open Tennis Championships | Date: August 26 – September 8, 2024

The [US Open Tennis Championships](https://www.usopen.org/en_US/tickets/index.html) captivate audiences with world-class tennis action. Held at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center, this Grand Slam event features thrilling matches as top players compete for tennis glory on the iconic hard courts of Flushing Meadows.

### 8. New York City Marathon | Date: November 3, 2024

The New York City Marathon, spanning several weeks in early 2024, is a world-renowned race attracting elite and amateur runners alike. Participants traverse the city’s diverse neighborhoods, showcasing the spirit of resilience and community. The marathon is an unparalleled experience, culminating in a celebration of accomplishment at the finish line in Central Park.
