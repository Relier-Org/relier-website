## Understanding Vehicle Options

Sedans offer a compact choice for solo executives or couples, accommodating up to four passengers with space for luggage in standard NYC itineraries. These vehicles suit airport transfers or quick meetings, providing a discreet profile for premium travel through crowded streets. Travelers value their maneuverability in tight urban spaces.

SUVs extend capacity to five or six passengers plus bags, ideal for small groups heading to events or multiple destinations. They deliver added comfort on longer rides, such as from [JFK to Midtown](https://relier.com/airport-to-hotel-transportation/), without sacrificing speed. This option balances space and efficiency for business or leisure.

Sprinter vans handle larger parties of up to 10-14, perfect for corporate teams or family outings spanning the city. With ample room for equipment or extended travel, they minimize the need for multiple vehicles during high-demand periods like upcoming festivals. Groups benefit from the consolidated transport.

## Factors in Fleet Selection

Group size remains the primary consideration; a sedan suffices for one to three people, while SUVs fit four to six, and Sprinter vans serve seven or more. Luggage volume also influences choices: sedans manage two to four bags, SUVs up to six, and vans far exceed that. Itineraries with multiple stops favor versatile SUVs for flexibility.

Comfort levels vary by duration and purpose. Short hops under an hour work well with sedans, but extended tours or events call for SUVs’ legroom or vans’ amenities like tables. Clients planning [hourly chauffeur services](https://relier.com/services/) should match vehicle scale to activity intensity.

- Assess passenger count and bags first.
- Factor in trip length and stops.
- Consider special needs like equipment space.
